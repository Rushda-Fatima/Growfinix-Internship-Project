# Task 4 – Travel Demand & Booking Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my Growfinix Internship.

The objective of this task was to analyze travel enquiry and booking data and create an interactive Tableau dashboard to identify booking trends, customer demand, conversion performance, travel patterns, and future booking demand.

The project involved data analysis, calculated fields, KPI creation, visualization, and forecasting using Tableau.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall travel enquiries
- Identify confirmed bookings
- Calculate enquiry-to-booking conversion rate
- Analyze booking trends over time
- Identify peak booking periods
- Analyze bookings by country
- Analyze quarterly booking patterns
- Understand enquiry-to-check-in lead time
- Forecast future booking demand

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

**Dataset:** Expedia Travel Dataset

**File:** `travel.csv`

The dataset contains travel search and booking-related information such as:

- Search date and time
- User location
- Check-in date
- Check-out date
- Booking status
- Travel-related attributes
- User and hotel information

The dataset was cleaned and prepared before visualization.

## 📊 Dashboard KPIs

### 1. Total Enquiries

Shows the overall number of travel enquiries/searches.

### 2. Confirmed Bookings

Shows the total number of successfully booked searches.

### 3. Conversion Rate

Measures the percentage of enquiries that resulted in bookings.

### 4. Peak Booking Month

Identifies the month with the highest number of confirmed bookings.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### Enquiries & Bookings – Actual vs Forecast

Shows the historical enquiry and booking trend along with forecasted demand.

### Enquiry to Check-in Lead Time

Shows the distribution of the time between enquiry/search and planned check-in.

### Top 10 Countries by Confirmed Bookings

Highlights the countries generating the highest number of confirmed bookings.

### Bookings by Quarter

Shows quarterly booking patterns and helps identify seasonal demand.

---

## 🔮 Forecasting

Tableau's forecasting feature was used to estimate future booking demand based on historical trends.

Historical data and forecasted values are visually differentiated in the dashboard.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Travel enquiry and booking activity varies across different periods.
- A relatively small proportion of enquiries convert into confirmed bookings.
- Certain countries contribute significantly more confirmed bookings than others.
- Booking activity shows noticeable quarterly/seasonal patterns.
- Lead time between enquiry and check-in varies across customers.
- Forecasting helps estimate future booking demand based on historical trends.
